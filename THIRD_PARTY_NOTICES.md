# Third-party dependencies

No third-party detector source code or pretrained checkpoints are vendored.
Detector loading/training/inference uses the separately installed Ultralytics
package. Its licensing terms apply independently; consult its AGPL-3.0 and
enterprise license information before distributing combined or modified works:

https://www.ultralytics.com/license

PyTorch, NumPy, scikit-learn, Pillow, PyYAML and matplotlib retain their respective
upstream licenses. Installation does not transfer ownership or permit removal
of upstream notices. The original authors must choose and approve the license
for this repository's own code before representing it as licensed open source.

The detector is described in: Zhao Y, Lv W, Xu S, et al. DETRs Beat YOLOs on
Real-time Object Detection. CVPR 2024, 16965-16974.
https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR52733.2024.01605
